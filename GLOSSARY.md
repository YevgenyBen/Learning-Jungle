# Learning Jungle

A Hebrew-only collection of learning games for children around first-grade level, covering Hebrew reading and math, made by Kiska & Kot Interactive. ("Learning Jungle" is a working name.)

## People

**Player**:
The child playing a Game.
_Avoid_: User, kid, learner, student

**Parent**:
An adult (parent or teacher) who may help the Player find their way through the Menus and choose settings.
_Avoid_: Admin, teacher, user

## Games

**Game**:
A self-contained learning activity that teaches one skill and belongs to exactly one Subject.
_Avoid_: Activity, exercise, level, app

**Subject**:
One of the two areas the app teaches: Hebrew or Math.
_Avoid_: Category, topic, course

**Menu**:
A screen for choosing a Subject or a Game, where every choice has a big, bright icon.
_Avoid_: Lobby, hub, launcher

**Game Frame**:
The shared surround of every Game, holding the same controls in the same places: back to Menu, pause, restart, mute, Niqqud and Difficulty.
_Avoid_: Wrapper, chrome, HUD, toolbar

**Round**:
A fixed set of Challenges in a Game, ending with a Celebration and the choice to play another Round.
_Avoid_: Session, level, game

**Challenge**:
One task within a Round, such as one word to build or one exercise to answer.
_Avoid_: Question, task, turn, exercise

**Choice**:
One of the few large options a Player picks from to answer a Challenge.
_Avoid_: Option, answer, button

**Hint**:
Help the Game gives after the Player has made repeated mistakes on the same Challenge, such as highlighting the right Choice.
_Avoid_: Clue, tip, help

**Celebration**:
The joyful animation and sound when the Player completes a Challenge or a Round.
_Avoid_: Reward, win screen, party

**Difficulty**:
How hard a Game is, chosen from that Game's own options; its "automatic" option adjusts as the Player succeeds.
_Avoid_: Level, stage, mode

## Content

**Content Bank**:
The single source of truth for all learning content, such as Words, letters and numbers, together with their pictures and Voice Clips.
_Avoid_: Word list, dictionary, database

**Word**:
A Hebrew word in the Content Bank, with its Voweled Spelling, Plain Spelling, picture, Voice Clip and Category.
_Avoid_: Item, entry, vocab

**Voweled Spelling**:
A Word written with Niqqud, using the spelling Hebrew takes when voweled (עַגְבָנִיָּה).
_Avoid_: Niqqud spelling, defective spelling

**Plain Spelling**:
A Word written without Niqqud, in standard full spelling (עגבנייה); not simply the Voweled Spelling with its marks removed.
_Avoid_: Unvoweled spelling, stripped spelling

**Letter Name**:
The spoken name of a Hebrew letter, such as "גימל".
_Avoid_: Letter label

**Picture**:
The image that stands for a Word; replaceable without changing any Game.
_Avoid_: Icon, emoji, image, illustration

**Letter Sound**:
A letter as written together with its vowel mark, such as בָּ, and how it is read aloud ("ba"). בָּ and בַּ are different Letter Sounds that sound the same.
_Avoid_: Syllable, phoneme

**Category**:
A grouping of Words by meaning, such as fruits or vegetables.
_Avoid_: Subject, topic, group

**Niqqud**:
The Hebrew vowel marks, which the Player can show or hide in each Game.
_Avoid_: Vowels, nikud, nekudot

## Sound

**Voice Clip**:
A prepared audio file of a Letter Name, Letter Sound, Word, number, spoken instruction or praise.
_Avoid_: Recording, audio, sound

**Speech Fallback**:
Reading text aloud with the device's own voice when no Voice Clip exists for it.
_Avoid_: TTS, synthesis

**Sound Effect**:
A short non-speech sound, such as a jump or a success chime.
_Avoid_: Beep, SFX, audio
